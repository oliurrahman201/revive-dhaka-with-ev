# End the Era of ICE Vehicles: Revive Dhaka with EV

A research-based academic project focused on reducing air pollution in Dhaka
by promoting Electric Vehicles (EVs) as a sustainable alternative to
Internal Combustion Engine Vehicles (ICEVs).

## Project Overview

Dhaka faces serious air pollution, traffic congestion, fuel dependency,
noise pollution, and climate-related challenges due to the increasing use
of Internal Combustion Engine Vehicles (ICEVs).

This project analyzes the environmental impact of ICE vehicles and presents
Electric Vehicles (EVs) as a cleaner and more sustainable transportation
solution for Dhaka.

## Objectives

1. To analyze the impact of Internal Combustion Engine Vehicles (ICEVs) on
   air pollution in Dhaka and present Electric Vehicles (EVs) as a
   sustainable solution to reduce emissions.

2. To provide a clear roadmap for reducing air pollution and making Dhaka
   a greener, cleaner, and healthier city by replacing ICE vehicles with EVs.

## Problems Identified

- Severe Air Pollution
- Traffic Congestion
- High Fuel Costs
- Noise Pollution
- Health Hazards
- Climate Impact
- Outdated Technology

## Proposed Solutions

### 1. Adopting EVs

Increasing the use of EVs can reduce carbon emissions and air pollution
because EVs produce zero tailpipe emissions.

### 2. EV Charging Stations

Charging stations should be installed in commercial areas, residential
zones, parking lots, and highways to improve accessibility.

### 3. Renewable Energy for EV Charging

Renewable energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power can
be integrated into EV charging infrastructure.

### 4. Off-Peak Charging

Bangladesh has excess electricity generation during some off-peak hours.
This electricity can be used for EV charging to reduce pressure on the
power grid.

### 5. Government Policies and Subsidies

Tax reductions, low-interest loans, and subsidies can encourage people
to switch from ICE vehicles to EVs.

### 6. Public Awareness

Workshops, advertisements, and social media campaigns can increase public
awareness and encourage EV adoption.

## Cost Difference Analysis

The project includes a comparison of fuel costs, power consumption,
vehicle costs, and potential profit/savings between ICE vehicles and EVs.
